Welcome! I’m **Carlo Musolino**, a PhD candidate in theoretical astrophysics at Goethe University Frankfurt, working in the group led by Prof. Luciano Rezzolla.

My research focuses on the physics of compact stars, with a particular emphasis on **binary neutron star mergers**, **gravitational waves**, and their **electromagnetic counterparts**. I specialize in the development of high-performance simulation tools for numerical relativity and multi-messenger astrophysics, including neutrino transport, relativistic hydrodynamics, and magnetohydrodynamics.

I'm especially interested in the post-merger phase of neutron star collisions and how advanced microphysics can shape observable signals such as short gamma-ray bursts and kilonovae. I’m also the lead developer of **GRACE**, a GPU-accelerated simulation framework for relativistic astrophysics.
